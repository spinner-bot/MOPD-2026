## Quality Criteria (from protocol §16)

Rate each criterion on a scale of 0-2:
- **0** = Clearly fails
- **1** = Marginal/uncertain
- **2** = Clearly passes

### 1. Answerability (必须高)
Can each guiding question be reliably answered from the image (or from previous guide's answer)?

### 2. Logical Dependency (至少中等)
Does each guide logically depend on previous guides? Is the chain structure valid?

### 3. Relevance (必须高)
Are all guides on the reasoning path to the final answer? No irrelevant hints?

### 4. Answer Leakage (必须为0)
Does any guide accidentally reveal the final answer or its key attributes?

### 5. Ambiguity
Is there any ambiguity in the guides or final question? Multiple valid interpretations?

### 6. Visual Grounding
Are all claims grounded in visual evidence from the image?

## Passing Criteria

A sample PASSES if:
- answerability >= 2
- relevance >= 2
- logical_dependency >= 1
- answer_leakage == 0
- final_answer is unique and unambiguous

A sample FAILS if any of these conditions are not met.

---

## Important Notes

1. Be STRICT about answer leakage - this is the most critical check
2. Consider both lexical leakage (answer word appears) and semantic leakage (answer is implied)
3. Verify that guides form a logical CHAIN, not just a list of hints
4. Check that the final answer can ONLY be determined by following the complete chain
