## 多人标注一致性

如果有两名标注者，计算 Cohen's κ：

| | 标注者 B: Pass | 标注者 B: Fail |
|---|:---:|:---:|
| **标注者 A: Pass** | a | b |
| **标注者 A: Fail** | c | d |

κ = (a+d - expected) / (total - expected)

**κ 值**: _____ 

- κ > 0.8: Almost perfect agreement
- κ > 0.6: Substantial agreement
- κ > 0.4: Moderate agreement
- κ < 0.4: Poor agreement (need clarification)
